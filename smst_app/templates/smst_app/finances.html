The statements

The book balances itself.

Every entry posts at least two lines into the chart of accounts. The profit and loss, the balance sheet and the cash flow are three views of the same posted lines. Nothing is totalled by hand.

  • Income, expenses, tax, discounts, refunds and losses keep their own accounts.
  • Every entry keeps its reference, date and the person who posted it.
  • Void an entry and the statements follow. The audit line stays.

Rules, set once

Tax, discounts, fees and refunds stop being a guess.

A finance option is a rule: a percentage or a fixed amount, applied to sales, purchases, stock movements or expenses. Set it once and the same rule is used every time an entry touches it.
